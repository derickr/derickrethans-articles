Friday Night Dinner: Oorja
==========================

This new location for Oorja is at the bottom of Marylebone High Street, a
little out of the way. We visited during their soft launch, which brought some
teething issues. More about that later.

Curiously each diner is provided with a fabric apron to wear, to protect their
clothes which was a bit of a novelty.

We had some popadoms when we arrived, and we have an issue with them. There
were only three actual popadoms; the rest (majority) were star shaped piped
"crisps". These reminded us slightly of an M&S packet of crisps with different
shapes — although I'm sure they were made in house. Traditional popadoms from
an Indian restaurant are wonderful things, thin and crispy, but although the
sauces were great, this box wasn't really as good as that.

The food generally was lovely. My wife ordered the black daal, which she
thought was amazingly tasty. The railway chicken curry that I had was also
delicious. A particular shout out for the cheese and onion bhajis that we had
as our starters — we're not sure that we ever had an onion bhaji with cheese,
but it worked well. But as usual, they came in portions of three — most
parties are two, which makes sharing them harder.

But onto the teething problems. The circular table at the top of the stairs
that we were sat at, was too small for two to dine comfortably. When we
arrived there were plenty of other tables for two free, and which remained
free during our whole visit. We felt like being squeezed in as a "walk in",
rather than for someone who had booked.

Service was attentive but not intrusive. Empty plates etc., were removed
swiftly. The pacing of courses was also good; not too, fast or too slow.

Desserts weren't available, so no comments on that. Overall we'd recommend
this place, and I hope it does well.
