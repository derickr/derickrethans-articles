Friday Night Dinner: Zylia
==========================

The former TGI Fridays site in Covent Garden has been replaced by a food and
cocktail court arrangement. There is a coffee shop and pancake place, but also
a few restaurants around the perimeter with their own entrance. One of these
is Zylia, a Greek/Cypriot restaurant.

Most of the dishes on the menu are meant for the table, so going with the
sharing approach we opted for a selection of cold and warm Mezedakia to start.
Their Taramosalata was probably one of the best variants that we've had. It
wasn't oily, or overly salty, but thick and flavourful. Some of the bread that
we ordered with it had the resemblance of a supermarket burger bun sliced
vertically, oiled and seeded. It was also a bit salty, and the pittas served
alongside were better.

We also shared the Spanakopita stack, consisting of crispy layers of filo
pastry, with generous dollops of  soft feta and spinach. The fried cheese
croquettes were ball-shaped, and super hot fresh out of the fryer. They came
with a sticky honey sauce.

From the Grill, we shared the Sheftalia (caul fat-wrapped pork parcels, think
koftas), and the Chicken Souvlakia — marinated chicken thighs with lemon oil
and oregano. Both meats were nicely cooked, and also full of flavour.

It is a bustling place, with a young audience. But it was also just a bit too
noisy, to the point where it was difficult to have a conversation. The food
however is delightful, and our five selected dishes were spot on quantity
wise.
