Friday Night Dinner: Marjorie's
===============================

A wine bar tucked around the corner of Carnaby Street. The restaurant is
narrow, and although they have a few tables for small groups, many of the
tables are larger and are seating multiple parties. It was a bit of a surprise
when we got seated downstairs at a large round table, with space for 8 people.
When we arrived, 6 other diners (all couples) were enjoying their meals.

Because it is a wine bar, the wines are on the expensive side. We picked a
lovely claret for just under £50 to go with our meal.

To start we ordered some bread, and brown butter, and a saucisson with some
cournichons, and a slice of the squash tart. The tart looked almost like a
slice of pizza, but had nice sweet squash flavoured layers interspersed with
cream and cheese, on a slightly crunchy base.

For my main, I picked the Mackerel, with pumpkin, pickles, and herbs. The
combination was a little on the sour side for me, but the fish was well cooked
and prepared. My wife chose the Bavette steak which was served with an egg
york emulsion — a similar idea to the egg yolk that often sits atop a steak
tartare. It was well seasoned, but slightly under for the medium that she'd
ordered.

Curiously, we did have to ask for the bread and saucisson a few times, and
these only arrived after our mains were served. This was disappointing, but
overall the meal, and the ambience were both lovely. There was definitely more
legroom than at the Barbary.
