Friday Night Dinner: Co & Ko
============================

Situated in SOHO, this small restaurant has a large selection of Korean
dishes. We didn't fancy the warm soups and broths on such a warm day, but
instead opted for the traditional grilled meats. We're sure that the broths
would work very well in the autumn or winter.

As our starter we shared a bowl of K Pop Chicken, which turned out to be
crispy cubes of chicken with a sweet sauce. For our mains, my wife enjoyed a
classic Lamb Bulgogi — pan-fried thin-sliced marinated lamb. I opted for
something new from their menu, the Bossam — a 12-hour slow cooked and grilled
Pork belly.

One of the reasons why we chose Korean cuisine for this evening, was to try a
Korean beer and a Makgeolli. The beer as part of our quest to try a beer from
each of the 48 participating World Cup countries. The Makgeolli because it was
a style of Korean drink that we had never previously tried.  We had the plain
variant at Co & Ko, and subsequently purchased a chestnut flavoured option
from one of the South Korean supermarkets that seem to be springing up in
London.

The food at Ko & Co was nice, and it had a good ambiance. On a hot summer
evening we were grateful to be sat in front of the open window. Judging by the
rest of the clientele, the restaurant seems to be popular with the Korean
community. There was a steady stream of diners coming and going, enjoying the
delicious looking food.

After our meal, we were still a little thirsty — it was a hot evening after
all — and shared a Soju. An enjoyable and relaxing way to finish our meal. We
would definitely return and recommend Co & Ko.
