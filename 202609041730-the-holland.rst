================================

It's a pub, not far from the Design Museum in Kensington, just off the main
road which forms High Street Kensington. The Holland is on a busy-ish road,
but inside it is a calm and relaxing spot. There are some tables outside too.
When we visited, it wasn't especially busy, with a couple of groups inside and
standing outside having some drinks in the warm weather. The staff were very
friendly and attentive, and before we knew it, we had our menus to pick our
dishes.

For her starter, my wife had the potted crab on toast. The portion size was
excellent, the crab tasty, and also nicely presented. I enjoyed the steak
tartare, which was mixed with tomato and a little horseradish. It was served
heaped with Parmesan cheese, and a potato cake (thin layers of potato, fried).

For my main, I had the "Fish of the Day" with a Vierge sauce and some spinach.
Today's fish was Pollock. It had a lovely crispy skin, and the sauce was
punchy with tomato, basil, and a large squeeze of lemon — maybe a little too
much though, which made it a touch too sour to my liking. My wife chose the
sweetcorn risotto. It was slightly sweet from the corn, with a touch of spice
and well cooked rice. The usual sprinkling of cheese added a bit of balance.

With our meal, we enjoyed a crisp Grüner Veltliner, by the bottle.

We did book this through "The Fork", which gave us £20 off as we had enough
"yums", which was nice! The Fork is one of the booking platforms we use to
find restaurants.
