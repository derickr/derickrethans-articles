Friday Night Dinner: Paris Bonheur
==================================

.. articleMetaData::
   :Where: 10 place du Maréchal Juin, 75017 Paris, France

We visited on a wet Thursday evening, and even though we ran from the metro
station around the square, we were still fairly damp by the time we arrived.
Inside, we were warmly welcome and seated in the window.

We started our meal with a glass of cremant, because why not. After selecting
our starters and mains, we asked the waiter for wine recommendations. We each
had different starters and mains, which called for something that suited each
dish. 

While we were waiting for our starters, we were presented with a stack of
home-made pretzels, and a cucumber gazpacho. I'm usually not a fan of the
original tomato version, but this variation with cucumber and tzatziki was
outstanding. The fresh cucumber with the yoghurt worked well, and little cubes
of perfectly even cucumber added a bit of crunch. One of the best dishes we
enjoyed this year.

For our starters, my wife had the fried squid and mussel salad, served with
some sweet grilled vine tomatoes. I enjoyed the pan-fried girolle mushrooms
and snails, finished with a vin jaune sauce. The sauce was rich and bound the
dish together. Some crunchy croutons added some texture.

For her main, my wife ordered the steak, and this is where Paris Bonheur let
us down a little. Although it was a good cut, it was sadly a little tough. The
fries were really good, and came freshly cooked and in a generous portion — if
only they had arrived with the steak as intended, rather than being served to
an adjacent table. This error was very quickly noticed and rectified. I chose
the smoked duckling with red fruits and beetroot as my main dish. The duck was
well cooked, although in my opinion the skin should have been crispy. The red
fruits added acidity, and the beetroot some welcome earthiness. 

We did not have a dessert, as we were too full! Overall we'd thoroughly
recommend this spot.
