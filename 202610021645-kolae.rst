Friday Night Dinner: Kolae
==========================

   We've been exploring the restaurants near Borough Market for a while now, and this time our eye fell on Kolae, a Thai Grill restaurant. The ground floor consists of a bar area, with a few tables sprinkled around. Our table was on the second floor, with about a dozen tables in an L-shaped room, with rough brick walls.

The menu is extensive, and as we didn't quite know what to pick, we went for
the easy option, and chose the set menu. We had the non-vegetarian set menu,
but we see the website has a vegetarian set menu too. Both were dairy free, if
that is a consideration.

The meal started with Biryani Rice Crackers with Pickled Ginger and Green Nahm
Jim. A stack of crispy and tangy crackers showed up, with hints of spice and
sour. For our starters, we were served Kolae Grilled Tiger Prawns. These had a
lovely sauce covered, and were served with some kind of fruit, with the
flavours of grape and citrus to squeeze on top. The Green Mango salad was also
spicy, but at the same time sweet, with dried shrimp adding some crunch. The
Soy-Braised Pork Belly showed up a while later — the kitchen had mixed
something up. The wait was well worth it, as this was in our opinion the best
dish on the menu. The sauce was sticky and rich, and the pork succulent and
tender. The sauce was so good. A spoon was deployed to not leave any of the
sauce behind. Due to the wait, we were also served a Chicken Bamboo Skewer,
which was also awesome, this is an optional extra on the set menu.

As our main course, the menu provided us with Stir Fried Farm Greens, with
some garlic and soybeans. At first, this looked really spicy due to the big
chilly on top, but it turned out fairly mild. It fit well together with the
Stonebass Curry, and the Hom Mali Rice.

We did not have space for dessert after this. It was a great meal, with
interesting flavours. The restaurant is in a good location, very close to the
Borough High Street exit of the London Bridge tube station. We would
definitely recommend it, with the only critique that it can be a bit noisy
upstairs if there is a loud group nearby.
