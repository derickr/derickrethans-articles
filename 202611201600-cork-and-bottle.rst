We visited Cork & Bottle before going to the theatre around the corner. It's
situated downstairs behind an unobtrusive door, right at the edge of busy
Leicester Square. Although mainly presents itself as a wine bar, they also to
classic British fare.

And that is what we choose. I enjoyed a Pork Belly with crispy skin, which was
served with rich dauphinoise potatoes and red cabbage and apply sauerkraut.
The skin was *really* crispy. My wife had the Shepherd's pie, with a creamy
potato layer and your traditional flavours. With this, we had a flight of
glasses of wine, as we couldn't pick one from their menu — there are so many!

We had a little time left, and thought we'd have a quick dessert in the form
of some chocolate truffles and a glass of sherry. That took a little longer
than we had hoped for, but the staff sorted it all out. After we enjoyed that,
we headed for the theatre.

A great spot, close to Leicester Square and many theatres. Good for an
after-theatre glass as well. We'll be back.
